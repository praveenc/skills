aws-deep-research v6.10

Evaluation of the multi-source research pipeline on design and performance, focused on your question: is there room for better wisdom extraction, insight, and synthesis? Findings are ordered by effort versus value.

Verdict

The orchestration architecture is genuinely strong — context isolation, contract grounding, facet decomposition, and silent-failure gating are best-in-class for an agent-dispatched researcher. The ceiling on quality today is not retrieval, it is the analysis layer: findings are compressed too early and blindly, synthesis is single-pass with no reflection, contradictions between sources are deduped rather than surfaced, and the pipeline never acts on the gaps it discovers. Those four are where the biggest wisdom gains live, and three of the four are prompt-only changes.

B What is excellent — keep as-is

Context isolation

Raw docs, pricing, and web content never enter the parent. Subagents write to disk; the synthesizer reads. This is the main reason it scales to 60–150 KB payloads without blowing context.

Research contract

A shared ground-truth artifact read first by every subagent, driving NOT/exclude query terms and version tagging. Excellent grounding and drift control.

Facet decomposition

Two to three facet-labeled subqueries per source, printed to the user before any credit is spent. Good coverage and transparency in one move.

Size-gate detector

The <500 byte check catches subagents that report success while writing a stub. Rare but real, and cheap to keep.

fetchv2 batching

Batched 10-URL fetch plus a truncation-recovery rule for primary sources kills the serial-trafilatura anti-pattern. Strong performance design.

Blind synthesizer eval

The Palmyra-versus-Claude spike was label-blind, byte-controlled, and run over two rounds. That rigor is rare — reuse the harness for the new work below.

D Prioritized recommendations

1 Surface contradictions, do not dedupe them
quick win		 High Low synthesizer.md Today step 4 says "deduplicate overlapping information." Insight lives in the tension — vendor claim versus community benchmark, docs versus field reports. Add a mandatory Consensus and Contradictions analysis plus a "where sources disagree" rule.
2 Add a "so-what" / decision-drivers layer
quick win		 High Low synthesizer.md format The report jumps from Detailed Findings to Recommendations with a thin analytical middle. Add Key Tensions / Trade-off drivers between them so second-order implications are explicit, not left to the model's prose instinct.
3 Confidence and recency tagging on every finding
quick win		 High Low researcher .md + contract rules Facet is tagged; source authority, date, and confidence are not. A one-token tag ([official·2026] or [community·unverified]) lets the synthesizer weight evidence and print confidence levels instead of flattening a 2021 blog and a 2026 doc into equal citations.
4 Reflection / critic pass on the draft report
big bet		 High Medium new critic agent Synthesis is single-pass read then write. A critic that scores the draft for unsupported leaps, shallow sections, and missed facets, then triggers a targeted revise, is the standard modern deep-research move and the highest-leverage depth upgrade after the quick wins.
5 Gap-driven follow-up research round
big bet		 High Med-high SKILL.md new step 6.5 The flow is linear: decompose once, dispatch, synthesize. Findings that reveal a critical unknown are only documented in Gaps, never acted on. Let the parent dispatch one or two targeted searches for the top gaps before synthesis. Single biggest lift for true deep research.
6 Two-tier findings: distilled plus raw evidence
big bet		 High Medium researcher .md + synthesizer Each researcher hard-compresses to 15 KB before the synthesizer sees anything — lossy compression by an agent blind to what the others found. Write a distilled file plus a raw-evidence appendix the synthesizer can pull on demand. Your own Palmyra spike flagged raw-ingest mode as unexplored.
7 Query-adaptive facet generation Medium Low search-strategy.md Facet pairs come from a fixed catalog. Have the parent propose facets tuned to the specific question first, falling back to the catalog. Minor gain — the current "craft one that fits" note already softens this.
8 Enforce web-budget as real state Medium Low scripts + .env Budget tracking is described ("should track cumulative") but not persisted. A tiny counter file makes the >80% switch-to-MCP rule actually fire instead of relying on the model to remember.
9 Automated synthesis-quality eval harness Medium Medium evals/ evals.json holds behavioral expectations, not scored rubrics with golden outputs. Add a graded rubric (coverage, insight density, citation accuracy, contradiction handling) so the quick wins above can be regression-tested, not just eyeballed.
10 Cross-session memory and reuse Medium High index over outputs/ Every session is standalone; a topic done last month is never consulted. Real but heavy — only worth it once volume justifies it.
11 Richer diagram types Low Medium diagram-generator.md Only D2 architecture and flow today. Comparison matrices, news timelines, and decision trees would visualize the insight layer, not just topology. Nice to have.

E The four that matter most

High valueLow effort

1 · Contradictions are deduped away

nowSynthesizer step 4: "Deduplicate overlapping information across sources." Overlap is treated as redundancy to remove. The real reports do tag vendor claims well ("vendor-reported, no third-party verification"), but that is manual prose discipline, not a systematic pass.

fixAdd a required step: cluster claims by topic, flag where sources disagree or where a vendor claim lacks independent confirmation, and emit a Consensus and Contradictions block. The disagreement is often the most decision-relevant thing in the report.

High valueLow effort

2 · The analytical middle is thin

nowReport arc is Executive Summary, then Detailed Findings, then Recommendations. The layer that turns facts into judgement — why these trade-offs matter, what actually drives the decision — is implicit, so quality rides entirely on the synthesizer model's prose skill. Your blind eval even showed the win came from "load-bearing extra words," meaning the model doing this in its head.

fixMake it explicit: a Key Tensions / Decision Drivers section between findings and recommendations. Forces second-order reasoning into the structure instead of leaving it to chance.

High valueMedium effort

4 · Single-pass synthesis, no reflection

nowThe synthesizer reads all findings and writes once. No self-critique, no "what is the strongest counter-argument," no check that every claim is supported. Shallow sections and unsupported leaps ship as-is.

fixA lightweight critic pass — a separate critic subagent scoring the draft against a rubric, or a mandated second synthesizer pass — then one targeted revision. Biggest depth lever once the two quick wins land.

High valueMed-high effort

5 · The pipeline never acts on the gaps it finds

nowThe flow is strictly linear. When a researcher surfaces a critical unknown, it lands in Gaps and Limitations and dies there. The sample report literally says "reviewing the full blog post directly is recommended" — a follow-up the system could have run itself.

fixInsert an optional step 6.5: after the size-gate, if a high-value gap is detected, dispatch one or two targeted follow-up searches (bounded, one extra round) before synthesis. This is what separates parallel search plus write-up from genuinely iterative deep research.
