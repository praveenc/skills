# Customer retention experiment: Q2 to Q3

**Date:** August 2026
**Source:** [Retention study](https://example.com/research/retention-study)

## Executive summary

The product team tested a guided onboarding sequence for new workspace
administrators. Thirty-day retention increased from 71% in Q2 to 78% in Q3.
The strongest improvement appeared among teams that invited at least three
members during their first session.

The result is promising but not conclusive. The Q3 cohort included more
enterprise customers than the Q2 cohort. The experiment also overlapped with a
pricing change, so the onboarding sequence cannot receive all credit for the
increase.

## Experiment design

- Q2 cohort: 4,120 new workspace administrators
- Q3 cohort: 4,380 new workspace administrators
- Primary measure: account active on day 30
- Secondary measure: three or more invited members within 14 days
- Test duration: 14 days of guided onboarding prompts

## Findings

Retention increased by 7 percentage points. The invite completion rate
increased from 46% to 61%. Teams that completed the invite step retained at a
higher rate in both cohorts.

The study does not establish that the onboarding sequence caused the full
retention increase. The next test should randomize accounts within the same
pricing period and report confidence intervals for each segment.
